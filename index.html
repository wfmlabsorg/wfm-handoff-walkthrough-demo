Walk-through · migration hand-offs

The forecaster hand-off, with nothing left unasked synthetic example

When a client moves between operations, one forecaster hands a methodology to another. The departure side holds years of history in a workbook; the arrival side builds a new plan in a new tool. The risk sits in the assumptions between them: constants typed into cells, rates borrowed from other clients, definitions nobody wrote down. This walk-through shows what an agent team does in the background to surface those assumptions, test them against what went wrong last time, and turn every gap into a question for the arrival forecaster, so both sides sign the same plan.

Why a CodespaceDrop the departure workbook into a private, company-controlled cloud workspace. The agents, the data and the audit trail sit in one place, with nothing to install, and a new workbook is analyzed minutes after it lands.
What the agents doFive roles: a data engineer that loads and repairs, a statistician that measures, a causal analyst that tests, a synthesizer that writes, and a coordinator that keeps owners, dates and gates.
What stays humanPurple steps need a person to sign before the next one runs. Every claim carries a status and a confidence in the ledger on the right; nothing low-confidence reaches a readout.
